How to play: Roll the dice, then select one or more open numbers whose total equals the roll. Shut those numbers and keep rolling. If no combination matches the roll, your turn ends and your score is the total of all numbers still open. A perfect board scores 0.

Multiplayer: Every player gets a fresh board using the same mode. After everyone has played, the lowest score wins. Ties are allowed.

Dice rule: Classic uses 2 dice for the entire game. Extended starts with 3 dice, drops to 2 when the open-number total is 12 or less, and drops to 1 when it is 6 or less.
