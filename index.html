How to play: Roll the dice, then select one or more open numbers whose total equals the roll. Click Shut Selected to close them and roll again. When the total of all open numbers is 6 or less, the game automatically switches to one die. If no combination can match the roll, the game ends and your score is the total of the numbers left open.
